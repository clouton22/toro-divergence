# Codex Project Instructions

This repository is the authoritative record for the Toro Divergence alternate-history project.

## Required reading order

Before answering a project-continuity question or proposing a new event, read:

1. `CANONICAL_TIMELINE.md`
2. `DECISIONS.md`
3. Any period-specific research files relevant to the request

Do not rely on conversational memory when it conflicts with the repository.

## Canon levels

- Material labeled **reviewed canon** or **Canonical** is stable.
- Material labeled **provisional** is the current intended direction but may be revised during its fifty-year review.
- Material labeled **candidate** is preserved for consideration and is not yet part of the timeline.
- Material labeled **rejected** should not be reintroduced without explaining what new cause resolves the earlier objection.

The review horizon in `CANONICAL_TIMELINE.md` identifies the last period that has completed a full plausibility review.

## Working method

When continuing a fifty-year review:

1. State the period and its main historical problem.
2. Establish a causal chronology.
3. Compare it with actual history.
4. Track effects in Iberia, Europe, and the Americas.
5. Trace credible reverberations in Africa, Asia, and the global economy.
6. Identify old threads paid off and new threads planted.
7. Test each divergence for political resistance, resources, timing, and unintended effects.
8. Finish by distinguishing newly settled canon from open questions.

Prefer causal ranges over unjustifiably precise dates. Preserve regional variation, especially among Indigenous societies.

## Continuity safeguards

- The Western Crowns begin as a composite monarchy, not a modern centralized state.
- Portugal's naval leadership and Castile's demographic weight remain a persistent tension.
- Brazil is known from around 1500 even though large-scale colonization is delayed.
- Epidemics remain severe; Indigenous resilience comes principally from staggered contact and institutional survival.
- Mixed Atlantic communities gain epidemiological experience rather than inherited blanket immunity; contact medicine develops empirically before germ theory.
- The European Reformation initially spreads and is contained only through later political events.
- American New Protestantism becomes Indigenous-authored and internally diverse.
- Hurricanes reinforce southern avoidance but cannot sustain it without naval, legal, economic, and military causes.
- Delayed slavery does not mean minor slavery.
- Historical thinkers, wars, and leaders may provide structural analogies, but should not be copied event for event.

## Editing the project

When the user asks to explore possibilities, do not silently promote the discussion to canon.

When the user explicitly adopts a decision:

1. Update the relevant timeline section.
2. Add or revise its numbered entry in `DECISIONS.md`.
3. Check downstream dependencies and terminology.
4. Advance the review horizon only after the complete period audit.

For substantial repository changes, prefer a focused branch and pull request unless the user requests a direct commit.
